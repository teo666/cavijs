D3InteractionController — d3-drag instead of hand-rolled pointer events

Same jack/plug/wire markup as the other patchbay demos, but driven by D3InteractionController (examples/d3-interaction.ts) instead of the library's default StandardInteractionController — a drop-in replacement built on d3-drag for gesture handling instead of hand-rolled pointerdown/pointermove/pointerup bookkeeping. Press and hold (no click-to-carry here — see the comment at the top of d3-interaction.ts) on a jack to pull a new cable, or on a plugged cable's plug to relocate it. Pan/zoom is d3-zoom, same as demo-patchbay-zoom.html — scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan; dragging a jack/plug is claimed by d3-drag instead (see the zoom behavior's .filter() in demo-d3-interaction.ts). Each dashed box below is its own independent .jack-unit — a plain draggable HTML element cavijs knows nothing about, dragged by its header via a separate d3-drag instance per unit (see makeDraggableUnit()). Plugged cables stay glued to a unit as you drag it, including the ones crossing between units.
