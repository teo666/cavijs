Two worlds on one page

Two independent <cavi-world>s, each with its own physics, cables and interaction. The second one lives inside a shadow root, and both use the same jack ids (in, out, aux): a cable's jack="id" is resolved among its own world's jacks only. Click a jack to pull a new cable, click another to plug it in.
