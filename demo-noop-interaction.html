No-op interaction controller

Try clicking/dragging the jacks or the plug below — nothing happens. This page assigns a <cavi-interaction> whose .controller is an IInteractionController with empty attach()/detach() (see src/exampleNoopInteraction.ts) instead of the StandardInteractionController that <cavi-world> would otherwise create automatically — proof that Jack/Plug install no listeners of their own and are entirely inert without some controller driving them.
